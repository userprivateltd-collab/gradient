Answers

Common Questions

What types of projects do you accept?
We specialize in native mobile applications (iOS/Android), complex web applications, custom UI/UX design, and specialized utility software. We prefer projects that demand high performance and premium aesthetics.
What is your typical timeline?
Timelines vary based on complexity. A standard web application or utility app typically takes 4 to 8 weeks from discovery to deployment, ensuring rigorous testing and design polish.
Do you handle both design and development?
Yes. We provide end-to-end services. Our methodology integrates UI motion design and engineering from day one, resulting in a cohesive, high-quality final product.
